hum

A personal assistant that one person runs on their own computer, for their own email, calendar and tasks.

hum watches its owner's inbox, calendar and task tracker, notices what needs attention, and writes a short daily brief. When something needs doing, it proposes a plan and asks before it acts. Every action that reaches another person waits for the owner's approval.

Who can use it

Only its owner. hum is not a hosted service: there is no sign-up and no account, and no one else can connect to it. It runs on the owner's machine and talks to the owner's own accounts.

What it connects to

  • Gmail: reads the sender, recipients, subject, labels and Gmail's short preview of inbox threads. It changes a thread's labels, for example to archive it, only when the owner approves.
  • Google Calendar: reads events, read-only.
  • ClickUp: reads the owner's tasks.
  • Telegram: sends the daily brief and approval requests to the owner's own chat.
  • Claude: Anthropic's models decide what matters and draft plans.

How hum handles this data is in the privacy policy.
